# Evidence gates

Most support loops are a company waiting for one piece of evidence it never asked for clearly. Supply it up front and the bot has nothing left to ask.

| Company type | Usually required before escalation |
|---|---|
| Software / apps | App + OS/browser version, exact steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual, **screen recording**, account email, error text or screenshot |
| Devices / hardware | Serial number, purchase date + receipt, photo/video of the fault, firmware version, what reset steps were tried |
| Subscriptions | Account email, plan name, dates of charges, cancellation confirmation (if any) |
| Telecom / ISP | Account number, service address, outage dates/times, speed test screenshots, prior ticket numbers |
| Banks / cards | Transaction date, amount, merchant, last 4 of card, what the merchant said |
| E-commerce | Order number, photos of item/packaging, tracking number, delivery date |
| Airlines | Confirmation code, flight number + date, what changed, receipts for extra expenses |
| Insurance | Claim number, denial letter, policy number, supporting documents |
| Healthcare billing | Account number, date of service, itemized bill, insurance EOB |

**Recording tips** (for software bugs): record the whole flow from the start, with the app's version visible if possible. Show the bad result at the end (play the broken file, show the error). Keep it under 2 minutes. Free tools: OBS, the built-in Windows Snipping Tool / Xbox Game Bar, macOS Cmd+Shift+5, or phone screen recording.
