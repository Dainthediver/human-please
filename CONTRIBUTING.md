# Contributing

The best contributions are real results. Open an issue or PR with:

- **Company type** (and the company, if you're comfortable naming it)
- **Channel:** email, chat, phone, social
- **What you sent:** remove personal info, account numbers, and phone numbers
- **What happened:** how many rounds, and what message actually got a human
- **Did the tool's draft help?** What would you change?

Patterns that show up across several reports go into `skills/escalate/references/triggers.md` and `PROMPT.md`.

**Please keep it factual.** Describe what happened, not opinions about the company or its staff. And no submissions about tricking or breaking support systems: this project is about honest messages that get routed correctly.
